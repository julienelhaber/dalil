A WhatsApp medicine companion for older patients in Lebanon, and for the family that looks after them from abroad.

This page replays Dalil's real messages, copied from its code, so you can try the flow without WhatsApp. The live bot runs on the WhatsApp Cloud API test number, which can only message approved phones. In the photo step, the page shows the result the box reader gives on our Flumivir photo instead of reading a new one.

What Dalil saved

Each step writes to Dalil's SQLite database on the laptop. These lines show what changed.

    Box reader: RapidOCR (16 MB, PP-OCRv4 models) on a laptop CPU, then fuzzy matching against the 5,762 drugs on the Lebanese Ministry of Public Health price list. On our test set it got 6 of 6 photos right, about half a second each, with Wi-Fi off.

    Dalil never changes a dose. It asks for consent first, the patient confirms every box, and when it is not sure it says so and points to the pharmacist. Georges and Rita are fictional; the medicines and prices are real entries from the official list.
